# Proposal

## Why

Scorecard checks 8 and 11 (`.impeccable/scorecard.md`) fail on the lead sentences. Impact opens on Today and, at 10 AM, says "24% self-powered today; this day last year was 38%": a morning against a whole day, with nothing saying the day isn't over. Impact's All time, and any range with no year before it, has no lead at all. A custom range that reaches today ("from Nov 1 to Nov 23") never says "so far". Solar has the same gaps: its spec already says a range starting today has no comparison, but a lone today is still compared with that whole date last year.

## What Changes

- **Today says "so far" and compares with nothing.** A range that starts today, Today included, gets a lead without a comparison on Solar and Impact: "24% self-powered today so far." Yesterday and earlier days keep their comparison with the same date last year, since both are whole days.
- **A custom range reaching today is named from its start.** "since Nov 1, 2026" in place of "from Nov 1 to Nov 23, 2026", on both pages, so the name says it runs up to now.
- **Solar's lead says "generated", in the tense of its range.** "Solar generated 1,086 kWh in October 2026" for a finished range, "Solar has generated 578 kWh in November so far" while it's under way, matching the "Solar generated" Impact and Top Ten already use.
- **"Generated" everywhere the site said "made".** Home's Today ("generated, of 18.0 kWh expected") and Since 2021 (legend, details and "Solar has generated as much as the house used"), the forecast card's hour legend and details and its "kWh generated by 1:40 PM" line, and Distribution's today-so-far panel.
- **Impact always has a lead.** With no year-earlier days to compare (All time, ranges in the first year, today), the lead gives the share alone: "41% self-powered since Oct 10, 2021." It used to give no sentence, which left All time without a figure in its heading.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `impact-report`: the lead sentence is always shown, a range starting today has no comparison, and a range without a year earlier gives the share alone.
- `solar-range-chart`: the lead says "generated" in its range's tense, names today "today so far" and a custom range reaching today by its start; a lone today has no comparison.
- `home-story`: Today's and Since 2021's wording says "generated".
- `solar-forecast`: the generated-against-expected line and the hour legend say "generated".
- `live-flow-scene`: Distribution's today-so-far panel labels solar "generated".

- No layout change, so no mockups: only the lead sentences' words change, in the header the last change set.
