## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Christmas lights on a phone
The bulbs SHALL stay small against the house: about 8 pixels tall on a 1440-pixel-wide desktop, and, where the scene is phone-sized, about 3 pixels tall, as their size before the desktop's reduction. They SHALL keep their places on the roof's edges at every width, with nothing scrolling sideways.

#### Scenario: A phone in the season
- **WHEN** the page is open at 9 PM on 2026-12-10 on a 390-pixel-wide screen
- **THEN** red and green bulbs show along the roof's edges, each about 3 pixels tall

#### Scenario: A desktop in the season
- **WHEN** the page is open at 9 PM on 2026-12-10 in a 1440-pixel-wide window
- **THEN** red and green bulbs show along the roof's edges, each about 8 pixels tall
